What does AKALUs Global do?

AKALUs Global is a development and consulting organization based in Jaipur, Rajasthan. We work with students, graduates, working professionals, academic institutions and organizations. Our support connects personal assessment, communication skills, professional profiles and preparation for career opportunities.
